Culinary Director & Founder

True culinary luxury is never about excess; it is the quiet alchemy of reverence for soil, flame, and the patient growers who sustain our heritage.

At Saffron Table, every grain and petal honors generations of craft. We source our crimson saffron directly from third-generation family co-operatives in Pampore, Kashmir and Khorasan. Each service is choreographed around our live open hearth, welcoming guests not merely to dinner, but to an unhurried communion of warmth and hospitality.
